# 10 Celeb Street Style Looks to Upgrade Your Everyday Wardrobe

Celebrity street style looks are constantly captured by photographers and fashion editors, offering an accessible blueprint for elevating everyday outfits. From supermodels and acclaimed actresses to top musicians, a well-curated off-duty outfit can quickly cement a star as a genuine trendsetter. Whether you want to master relaxed tailoring, chic denim, or bold outerwear, studying celeb street style provides endless inspiration for mixing casual basics with statement pieces. Here are ten iconic off-duty celebrities street style ensembles to inspire your wardrobe rotation.

## 1. Elizabeth Olsen

![Elizabeth Olsen](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/1_elizabeth-olsen_290x580.jpg)

Elizabeth Olsen has become celebrated for her sophisticated, effortlessly cool approach to everyday fashion. This ensemble featuring tailored cognac trousers, a crisp loose white shirt, a sleek leather vest, and classic menswear-inspired brogues oozes off-duty style. By mixing structured leather with soft cotton and tailored pants, she crafts a balanced silhouette that feels both modern and timeless.

## 2. Kate Bosworth

![Kate Bosworth](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/2_kate-bosworth_290x580.jpg)

Kate Bosworth is a perennial favorite for effortless festival and street fashion inspiration. Here, a sleeveless denim dress is accessorized in her signature minimalist aesthetic—complemented by a streamlined cross-body side bag and well-worn boots. If you want to elevate simple denim, take inspiration from [Elizabeth Olsen's menswear accessories](#1) or balance casual cotton dresses with rugged leather footwear for an intriguing contrast.

- **Key Staple:** Sleeveless denim shirt-dresses for versatile layering.

- **Footwear Match:** Weathered ankle boots to ground lightweight fabrics.

- **Accessory Accent:** Minimalist crossbody bags that keep hands free.

## 3. Kate Moss

![Kate Moss](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/3_kate-moss_290x580.jpg)

For a definitive lesson in styling animal prints without overwhelming an outfit, look no further than supermodel Kate Moss. As a legendary style icon in her own right, she demonstrates that a tailored leopard print coat serves as a perfect statement piece to liven up a dreary winter outfit. Pairing bold outerwear with simple dark trousers and boots ensures the print remains the focal point while keeping the overall look polished according to style features on [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com).

## 4. Nicole Richie

![Nicole Richie](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/4_nicole-richie_290x580.jpg)

Nicole Richie is a favorite of fashion photographers, so her outfits are well documented. This outfit of black skinny jeans, a tiger print sweater, and colored pumps is a lot more sophisticated than the boho celebrity street style looks she was once famous for. The oversized sunglasses, however, remain a mainstay that instantly pulls together any casual ensemble.

## 5. Miranda Kerr

![Miranda Kerr](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/5_miranda-kerr_290x580.jpg)

Miranda Kerr has the model-off-duty look down to a tee. Here she is between runway shows working an artfully layered look complete with killer leggings and sleek boots. Similar to [Kate Moss's statement coat](#3), thoughtful outer layers are key to transforming functional leggings into a fashion-forward street ensemble.

| Style Category | Key Wardrobe Piece | Styling Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tailored Layering | Leather Vest & Trousers | Mix structured leather with soft cotton shirts |
| Casual Denim | Sleeveless Denim Dress | Pair with rugged boots for texture contrast |
| Statement Outerwear | Leopard Print Coat | Keep base layers monochromatic and understated |

## 6. Taylor Swift

![Taylor Swift](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/6_taylor-swift_290x580.jpg)

She's the darling of the music industry and her outfit choices are usually just as sweet. This cute cat print skirt, however, has been toughened up with a motorcycle jacket and lace-up shoes. This is one of those celebrity street style looks that could be easily emulated for daily wear.

## 7. Alexa Chung

![Alexa Chung](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/7_alexa-chung_290x580.jpg)

There are entire websites dedicated to Alexa Chung's outfits. This style icon has definitely made a name for herself with contemporary fashion choices. This outfit of grey separates, leather-trimmed jacket, and Mary-Jane platforms is an excellent example of her offbeat, preppy style.

## 8. Olivia Palermo

![Olivia Palermo](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/8_olivia-palermo_290x580.jpg)

She made a name for herself on television, but now Olivia Palermo is just as well known for her impeccable style. Here she works a typical celebrity street style look of leather leggings and a fur vest. The color blocked sweater and burgundy ankle boots are a nice touch, creating a cohesive look similar to [Miranda Kerr's sleek proportions](#5).

- **Texture Mix:** Combine smooth leather with soft knits or faux fur.

- **Color Palette:** Pick one accent shade (like burgundy) across shoes and accessories.

- **Proportion Play:** Pair fitted bottoms with voluminous tops or vest layers.

## 9. Liv Tyler

![Liv Tyler](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/9_liv-tyler_290x580.jpg)

This outfit that Liv Tyler is wearing is feminine but with a little bit of a rock edge. The white lace tunic really stands out against black separates. Like most celebrity street style looks, dark sunglasses are a must for adding effortless cool.

## 10. Rosie Huntington-Whitely

![Rosie Huntington-Whitely](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/celebs/2012/05/10_rosie-huntington-whitely_290x580.jpg)

In a look straight off the runways, Rosie Huntington-Whitely wears a bevy of designer threads. Usually well known for her model-off-duty style of jeans and biker jackets, here she gives a chic [Stella McCartney](https://www.stellamccartney.com) dress a bit of edge by teaming it with [Alexander Wang](https://www.alexanderwang.com) heels.

These are only a few examples of what stylish celebrities have been wearing lately. Celebrity street style looks are a great way to have a peek at what celebrities are wearing when they are not being styled for the red carpet. What is your favorite celebrity street style look?
