## 2. THEY RAN a HOTEL TOGETHER in TEL AVIV until 2015

![Photograph, Forehead, Black-and-white, Love, Interaction,](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qv/br/vkj0jyhn5da9f8a9a1309624224273_600x600.jpg)

Versano is a successful real estate mogul and entrepreneur. Before moving full-time into film production ventures, Versano, his brother, and Gadot co-owned and operated the highly-rated Varsano Hotel in the historic Neve Tzedek neighborhood of Tel Aviv. Gadot admitted in past interviews that they were actively involved in the daily operations of the boutique hotel, even helping adjust bed linens themselves. However, in 2015, Versano and his partners sold the luxury property to Russian billionaire Roman Abramovich for approximately 26 million USD.
