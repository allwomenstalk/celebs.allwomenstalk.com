# 9 of the Biggest Triple Threats in Hollywood: Famous Performers Who Can Do It All

There are tons of superbly talented people in Tinseltown, but true triple threats in Hollywood are one in a million. When people ask what is a triple threat in hollywood, it refers to performers who can act, sing, and dance with exceptional skill. To me, a true triple threat is someone who can do so many things so well that you wouldn't even know what to identify them with most! Be it singer, actor, model, designer, or dancer, you can’t just pin them down because they are so remarkably talented at everything. Below are 9 of the biggest and most famous triple threats in Hollywood history who continue to set the standard for modern entertainment.

## 1. Jennifer Lopez

![Jennifer Lopez](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/tu/wy/ox/azg98bva_600x763.jpg)

Without a doubt, one of the hottest female triple threats in Hollywood is our favourite Jenny from the block. She started out as a dancer on _In Living Color_ and broke her way into major films. Often, we forget that J.Lo established herself in acting before she fully ventured into the music business. Then she sang so well that she became one of the best-selling female artists in music history. As an actress (we all watched [The Wedding Planner](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0209475/), right?), musician ("Dance Again" stayed at the top of the chart for weeks), and an incredible dancer, we should also keep in mind she has her clothing line and signature perfumes. She is definitely one of the ultimate triple threat female celebrities.

Key attributes that define famous triple threats include:

- Versatile vocal range and chart-topping musical releases
- Dynamic acting skills across comedy, drama, and action
- Professional dance background and high-energy stage presence

## 2. Justin Timberlake

![Justin Timberlake](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qg/sz/jr/bfo2ml2b_600x899.jpg)

JT is amazing, and that is an absolute fact. He can sing, he can act, he can dance, and he is an incredible songwriter and fashion designer. NSYNC was one of the biggest boy bands in the world, and now he is one of the biggest solo artists globally. His role in _The Social Network_ put him on the list of serious actors who are triple threats, and we all know he can move effortlessly on stage. His fashion label William Rast has been featured in department stores across North America, plus he expanded into hospitality with his restaurant Southern Hospitality. Hats off to Justin, hands down one of the best triple threats of all time!

## 3. Eminem

![Eminem](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/n6/n5/os/o43okvh7_600x822.jpg)

Eminem isn’t usually someone who would immediately appear on a standard list of triple threat actors, but think about it carefully. He is obviously an incredible rapper, a genius songwriter, and he proved he can act brilliantly in _8 Mile_. Plus, he is a record producer who runs Shady Records, all while overseeing major hip-hop careers, being a dedicated father, and achieving personal sobriety. Give it up to this Detroit-born superstar for defining what a unique triple threat celebrity can be.

## 4. The Entire Cast of Glee

![The Entire Cast of Glee](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/pp/sc/m8/tw4upywn_500x614.jpg)

Every single one of the performers on _Glee_ deserves to be on the list of the biggest triple threats in Hollywood. From the very first episode, viewers were blown away by how multi-talented the ensemble was. They had to sing, act, and dance at a professional level within the tight time frame of weekly television production. Memorizing complex dance numbers, recording vocals, and mastering script lines all at once requires astounding skill and stamina.

## 5. Kate Hudson

![Kate Hudson](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fk/dl/wa/n24hmwl5_600x816.jpg)

Kate Hudson is utterly fantastic and super gorgeous. For a while, audiences thought of her primarily as a great feature film actress, but her performance in the musical film _Nine_ left everyone floored. She has an incredible singing voice, and her guest dance number on _Glee_ (the "Dance Again / Americano" mashup alongside other [triple threat performers](#4)) was unforgettable. Cast members even remarked it was like watching Britney Spears in her heyday. Singing, dancing, and acting come naturally to her, making Kate one of the sexiest triple threats in Hollywood.

## 6. Victoria Beckham

![Victoria Beckham](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fw/mq/yb/htn7jjnr_600x808.jpg)

Who doesn’t love Victoria Beckham? As Posh Spice of the Spice Girls, we all know she can sing and perform on international stages, but she also excels as an acclaimed fashion designer. You know you’re one of the biggest triple threats in Hollywood when you transition from global pop star to high-fashion icon with supermodels wearing your runway collections. VB built an empire brand, showing how multifaceted talent can evolve beyond the traditional stage.

## 7. Zac Efron

![Zac Efron](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/vd/j7/mb/aayagamo_600x900.jpg)

Zac Efron has navigated a challenging Hollywood path, successfully transitioning from a teen musical heartthrob into a versatile A-list actor. He possesses a great singing voice, dance moves that impress audiences worldwide, and dramatic and comedic acting chops. Proving that modern [pop star and musical transitions](#2) can yield serious Hollywood staying power, Zac remains one of the most prominent triple threat actors of his generation.

## 8. Neil Patrick Harris

![Neil Patrick Harris](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/xx/la/j1/0wbzmn4n_600x858.jpg)

Neil Patrick Harris was blessed with being one of the most multi-talented entertainers to ever grace Hollywood. NPH is an incredible actor with Primetime Emmy Awards to attest to his versatility, a seasoned [Tony Award](https://www.tonyawards.com)-winning Broadway veteran, and a master host who has won awards specifically for hosting major live award shows. His rare combination of stage magnetism, comedic timing, and musical talent makes him one of the ultimate triple threats in Hollywood.

What sets elite triple threats apart in live performance:

- Seamless transition between television acting and live theatre
- Exceptional hosting ability and comedic timing
- Vocal proficiency across musical theatre and pop standards

## 9. Hugh Jackman

![Hugh Jackman](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ae/h4/m4/tcgb0ak8_600x864.jpg)

This talented Aussie is one of the sexiest triple threats in Hollywood. Hugh Jackman has frequently shared how much he loves live theatre, and whenever he sings and dances on stage, audiences are captivated. He can dominate action blockbusters as Wolverine and seamlessly transition into grand musical cinema like _Les Misérables_, earning an [Oscar nomination](https://www.oscars.org) for his performance. Joining the ranks of legendary [Broadway veterans](#8) who excel on screen, Hugh Jackman proves just how extraordinary multi-talented stars can be.

It is incredibly challenging to excel in one area of entertainment, but to be in high demand across singing, acting, and dancing is a rare feat. Seeing these famous triple threats perform is both inspiring and motivating. Whether in film, music, or theatre, multi-talented performers continue to push artistic boundaries. Who do you think ranks among Hollywood's greatest triple threats?
