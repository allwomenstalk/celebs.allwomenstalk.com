# 7 Celebs from ABC's Scandal Who Love to Live\-Tweet

There are so many celebs from ABC's drama [Scandal](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1837576/) who love to live-tweet through new episodes. It's actually so funny to see what the actors have to say about the intense scenes in real time. If you've kept up with the series created by Shonda Rhimes, then you know just how crazy each season gets. Way to keep fans biting their nails all season long! The finale episodes are always prime time to see all of these stars taking social media by storm. Check out which cast members make watching the show even more thrilling!

- Hilarious live reactions to major plot twists and cliffhangers

All hail Olivia Pope! Everything she does is flawless. Am I right, ladies? I love when Kerry and her team tweet during the show. She even tries to reply to fans as much as she can! Kerry's also constantly showing love to her co-stars, praising their talents on and off screen, including fellow cast members like [Katie Lowes](#2) and [Tony Goldwyn](#5). If you don't already do so, you need to be following her official profile at [@kerrywashington](https://x.com/kerrywashington)! She's just one of the standout stars from ABC's Scandal who are always live-tweeting.

## 2. Katie Lowes

![Katie Lowes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ys/wr/ov3p4bi2_600x818.jpg)

Remember when Katie played good-girl Quinn? Those days are long behind her, but she's still one of the most compelling characters on the show! Katie's a fantastic actress who brings so much energy to her role. She's always tweeting through new episodes, especially when a steamy scene with [Guillermo Diaz](#7) airs! It's hilarious to see her genuine thoughts on those plot turns. You can follow her updates at [@KatieQLowes](https://x.com/KatieQLowes)!

## 3. Darby Stanchfield

![Darby Stanchfield](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ps/sl/cym13x0g_600x711.jpg)

Darby is easily one of the most entertaining Scandal stars to follow online. If you've witnessed one of her live-tweeting sessions, then you know how hilarious she really is. She's always posting play-by-play commentary during episode airings with wonderfully witty remarks. She even playfully requests that co-star [Scott Foley](#4)'s character Jake has his shirt off 24/7. You can follow her live reactions at [@DarbySOfficial](https://x.com/DarbySOfficial)!

## 4. Scott Foley
