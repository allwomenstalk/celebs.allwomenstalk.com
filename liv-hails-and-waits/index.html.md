# Liv Tyler Street Style: How to Elevate Her Casual NYC Outfit

When it comes to effortlessly chic star fashion, [Liv Tyler](https://celebs.allwomenstalk.com/)’s off-duty looks always catch our eye. Spotted as she hails a taxi cab in the iconic [West Village](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Village) of [New York](https://allwomenstalk.com/popular/) City, her ensemble offers a classic baseline. You can't go wrong with black denim and a neutral top, but her basic leggings and tights feel a bit understated when paired with plain [black flats](https://shoes.allwomenstalk.com/). While we love a cozy everyday outfit, adding a bit more vibrant color would really elevate this [street style](https://streetstyle.allwomenstalk.com/) look!

Key elements of this effortless street style look:

- Dark denim layer for classic structure

- Black leggings and tights for comfortable mobility

- Casual plain black flats

- A vibrant pop of color from her signature handbag

City: New York

As featured in [Vogue](https://www.vogue.com) street style spotter guides, balancing neutrals with statement accessories is essential for urban fashion.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Denim Jacket Layer | Timeless casual staple | Add tailored layering or structured shoulders |
