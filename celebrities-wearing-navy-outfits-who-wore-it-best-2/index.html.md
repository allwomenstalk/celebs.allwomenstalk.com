# 9 Celebrities Wearing Navy Blue Dresses & Outfits on the Red Carpet

We’ve spotted a number of female celebrities in blue outfits lately, both on and off the red carpet. Navy blue is a timeless, sophisticated shade that often gets overlooked in favor of standard black. While it was once rare to see celebrities wearing navy blue dresses at major star-studded events, recent years have proven that navy creates an understated, incredibly chic impact. Whether styled for a high-glamour evening or paired for polished street style, navy blue dress celebrity fashion is having a major moment. Take a look at these iconic celebrities wearing navy blue dresses and chic black and navy outfits, and let us know which look is your favorite.

**Why Navy Blue Dresses Dominate Red Carpets:**

- **Versatile Elegance:** Delivers the slimming, formal allure of black with a richer visual depth.
- **Universally Flattering:** Complements virtually every skin tone under intense photoflash lighting.
- **Seamless Transition:** Adapts easily from ultra-glam galas to effortless off-duty streetwear.

## 1. Dianna Agron

![Dianna Agron wearing a navy Miu Miu dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204446_6172_550x1100.jpg)

First up on our list of celebrities wearing navy outfits is Dianna Agron. She expertly teams a navy [Miu Miu](https://www.miumiu.com) dress with an embellished black coat and cutout ankle booties. The color combination of black and navy is a timeless pairing (much like how [Alexa Chung styles her navy coat](#7)). Here, the bright blue clutch and embellished collar add fun, playful elements that elevate the overall look.

## 2. Amanda Seyfried

![Amanda Seyfried in navy dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204445_5804_550x1100.jpg)

If you previously thought of navy as a quiet or subtle color, think again. Amanda Seyfried stepped out in a stunning navy number that demands attention. The dress features delicate metallic polka dot details and is crafted from sheer chiffon fabric. She finishes her look with strappy black heels and a bold red lip, offering great inspiration for what shoes to wear with a navy dress when aiming for classic glamour.

## 3. Selena Gomez

![Selena Gomez wearing navy Atelier Versace](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204445_7400_550x1100.jpg)

Selena Gomez proves that navy is anything but quiet on the red carpet. Here she wears a striking navy [Atelier Versace](https://www.versace.com) gown featuring studded trims, a high thigh split, and a risqué bustier detail. This unforgettable Selena Gomez blue dress red carpet look showcased how midnight navy can deliver pure high-fashion drama.

## 4. Jennifer Lawrence

![Jennifer Lawrence in navy Dior gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204445_6980_550x1100.jpg)

Jennifer Lawrence is another leading lady who embraced navy for high-profile press events. She wore a dramatic [Christian Dior](https://www.dior.com) gown while promoting ‘The Hunger Games: Catching Fire’. The bandeau-style dress features an asymmetrical hemline and sheer skirt overlay. Paired with her textured cropped haircut and dark nail polish, the ensemble leans nicely into a rock-and-roll edge, standing alongside [Selena Gomez’s show-stopping gown](#3) as a top red carpet highlight.

## 5. Jennifer Garner

![Jennifer Garner wearing navy Vionnet gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204445_1418_550x1100.jpg)

Jennifer Garner looks radiant and fresh-faced wearing a gracefully draped navy gown by [Vionnet](https://www.vionnet.com). The dress relies on a clean column silhouette with gentle draping across the waist. Sparking jewelry and a vibrant pink lip bring an extra glow to her overall presentation.

## 6. Naomie Harris

![Naomie Harris wearing navy Vionnet gown](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204446_6283_550x1100.jpg)

Naomie Harris makes a bold statement in a plunging navy gown by Vionnet. Featuring sheer net accents and dramatic cape-like sleeves, the silhouette needs little extra styling. She keeps accessories minimal with a delicate bangle and a sleek box clutch, letting the architectural lines of the dress take center stage.

## 7. Alexa Chung

![Alexa Chung in navy coat](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204452_5774_550x1100.jpg)

A tailored navy coat is an essential wardrobe staple. Alexa Chung layers her structured navy coat over a black velvet mini dress for a timeless spin on black and navy outfits. T-bar heels, a chain-strap handbag, tousled hair, and minimal makeup complete this effortlessly cool aesthetic.

## 8. Miranda Kerr

![Miranda Kerr in navy coat and printed dress](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204452_4290_550x1000.jpg)

Miranda Kerr masters off-duty elegance by draping a navy coat over a patterned dress. The look feels refined when combined with classic nude pumps and a structured top-handle handbag. Matching red lipstick and polished nails bring cohesion to the entire ensemble. If you love layered street style like [Rosie Huntington-Whiteley's winter outfit](#9), this provides perfect inspiration.

## 9. Rosie Huntington-Whitely

![Rosie Huntington-Whitely in navy coat and jeans](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31204453_2781_592x888.jpg)

Rosie Huntington-Whiteley delivers classic model-off-duty style in tailored skinny jeans and a warm navy coat. Coupled with ankle boots and a structured tote, this casual yet elevated outfit is ideal for cool-weather dressing that anyone can recreate.

These celebrity looks demonstrate just how versatile navy blue dresses and outerwear can be. From dramatic red carpet gowns to everyday street style coats, navy remains one of fashion’s most chic choices. Which of these navy outfits is your personal favorite?
