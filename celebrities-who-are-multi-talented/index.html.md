# 11 Multi\-Talented Celebrities: Stars Who Are Actors, Singers, and Dancers

I've always been in awe of multi-talented celebrities who excel across multiple entertainment fields. I guess that happens when your own claims to fame include burping the alphabet and winning a college Pig-Latin poetry contest (“Eshay alksway inay eautybay ikelay ethay ightnay...” Forgive me, Lord Byron.) All kidding aside, there is something remarkable about discovering that a famous artist you admire is equally skilled in another part of the industry. From multi-talented actors who can sing to dancers who command the director's chair, these celebrities with multiple talents demonstrate extraordinary versatility. Managing a career like theirs takes immense dedication, but in these cases, the effort really pays off.

## 1. Justin Timberlake

![Justin Timberlake](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/u1/hx/itoy72hg_600x814.jpg)

I'll admit I wasn't much of a fan of 'N SYNC back in the day, but Justin tops my list of multi-talented celebrities. For one, he can sing and dance circles around a lot of other acts around today. He's genuinely funny with legendary appearances on [Saturday Night Live](https://www.nbc.com/saturday-night-live), he's a charismatic actor, and his modeling presence is undeniable. What I love most is how he never takes himself too seriously; a sharp sense of humor is an absolute must in my book. His skill set as a multi-talented performer mirrors the versatility seen in other [dancing performers](#6) on this list.

## 2. Prince

![Prince](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/aq/s5/pywpaqxh_600x910.jpg)

To say this Grammy and Oscar winner is a multi-talented artist is an understatement. The legend directed, produced music for himself and others, choreographed, played lead guitar, sang, danced, and taught himself how to play dozens of musical instruments. Dave Chappelle famously featured a legendary skit about Prince playing basketball on _Chappelle's Show_, and it turns out he really was a skilled high school basketball player too. He remains perhaps the most multi-talented person to ever grace the stage, setting a high standard for [multi-talented performers](#7) everywhere.

Here are a few key areas where Prince demonstrated his extraordinary versatility:

- Mastering over 27 musical instruments in the studio
- Composing, arranging, and producing hit tracks for himself and other artists
- Writing, directing, and starring in major feature films

## 3. Ron Howard

![Ron Howard](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/jv/jf/php8lzmq_600x807.jpg)

If I were to list all of his accomplishments in writing, acting, and directing, I'd be here all day. Instead, I'll let his extensive filmography on [IMDb](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000165/) detail his decades in Hollywood. On a side note, I think it's hilarious how Finn and Hodgins call each other “Opie” and “Thurston” on _Bones_. It's a great homage to his classic TV role, and a fun reference for fans of television history.

## 4. Eddie Murphy

![Eddie Murphy](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/kz/jv/sun4r1k5_600x897.jpg)

Say what you want about "Party All the Time," but Eddie Murphy is actually a remarkably solid singer. He did a short rendition of “I'm a Believer” at the end of _Shrek_, and his vocals were impressive. I absolutely love the way he plays multiple distinct characters within a single film, a signature move across his comedy classics. Beyond stand-up, he wrote, acted in, and directed _Harlem Nights_, alongside his iconic run on _Saturday Night Live_ in the early '80s. His comedy can be gritty, but it's just one facet of a multi-talented actor and performer.

## 5. Penny Marshall

![Penny Marshall](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/er/mv/cicc9zcz_600x882.jpg)

A lot of us know her for directing classics like _A League of Their Own_ and _Riding in Cars with Boys_, but she also built a massive acting career in the '70s and '80s. She starred as Laverne DeFazio in both _Happy Days_ and _Laverne & Shirley_, in addition to roles on _The Odd Couple_ and _The Mary Tyler Moore Show_. Laverne remains one of the most iconic sitcom characters of her era. It's truly inspiring to read about women who broke barriers and achieved monumental success in directing and acting alike.

## 6. Paula Abdul

![Paula Abdul](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ur/yp/stvd37ln_600x874.jpg)

Many younger fans know her from judging on _American Idol_, but she broke out as a premier singer, dancer, and choreographer. Before launching her solo pop career, she crafted legendary choreography for Janet Jackson. The music video for “Cold-Hearted Snake” showcases her unbelievable dance athleticism, while “Opposites Attract” seamlessly blended live-action dance with animation. Starting out as a Dallas Cowboys cheerleader, she proved early on that she belonged among top-tier multi-talented celebrities.

## 7. Patrick Swayze

![Patrick Swayze](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/rb/kw/fvtkzckq_600x750.jpg)

Remember “Dirty Dancing”? That movie was an absolute staple at slumber parties for a reason. Beyond his famous roles, Swayze was a classically trained dancer and a dramatic actor who brought real emotional depth to the screen. What surprised many fans is that he also co-wrote and sang “She's Like the Wind” for the movie's soundtrack. His vocals were smooth and heartfelt, proving his gifts extended far beyond dancing and acting. Much like [River Phoenix](#9), he possessed a rare musical sensibility to complement his acting stardom.

## 8. Christian Kane

![Christian Kane](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/md/u4/wz06sn0a_600x900.jpg)

Beyond his acting career, Christian Kane has built a dedicated following as a country singer and songwriter. On an episode of _Leverage_ involving songwriters, he performed his own track, “Thinking of You.” He has a soulful country vocal style that surprises viewers who know him primarily for playing tough, action-heavy roles on television.

Here is a quick snapshot of his multi-talented career highlights:

- Starring in popular action-drama series like _Leverage_ and _The Librarians_
- Fronting the country-rock band Kane and recording solo records
- Performing original music on-screen for his television projects

## 9. River Phoenix

![River Phoenix](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/z0/mr/tq3bv7i0_600x902.jpg)

When reflecting on gifted young actors, River Phoenix stands out for having a deeper well of talent than many realized. Beyond his acclaim as an actor, he was a passionate musician. He formed the alternative band Aleka's Attic with his sister Rain, and wrote and performed original music for _My Own Private Idaho_. His artistic ambition reflected that of [fellow musician and actor Justin Timberlake](#1), proving his artistic depth reached across mediums.

## 10. The Entire Cast of “Chicago”

![The Entire Cast of “Chicago”](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/qk/jr/gkswjm5n_600x758.jpg)

Some musical films rely on vocal dubbing, but the principal cast of _Chicago_ performed their own singing and dancing. Queen Latifah already had a celebrated rap and vocal background before acting, but Catherine Zeta-Jones surprised audiences with her show-stopping musical theater prowess. Richard Gere undertook five months of intensive tap dance lessons for the role, building on his existing musical foundation. Meanwhile, Renée Zellweger delivered an iconic performance as Roxie Hart without prior formal vocal or dance training.

## 11. Taylor Lautner

![Taylor Lautner](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/fr/et/kpyy3e8z_600x900.jpg)

Known worldwide for his role in the _Twilight_ saga, Taylor Lautner boasts impressive martial arts credentials outside of acting. Starting at age 8, he won three gold medals in the World Karate Association championships. As highlighted in a feature on [buzzfeed.com](https://www.buzzfeed.com/emilyhennen/25-famous-people-who-are-good-at-something-besides-being-fam), his martial arts background gives him an athletic discipline that sets him apart from conventional actors.

It is always fascinating to discover that an entertainer you admire possesses talents far beyond their primary career. In an industry full of quick fame, multi-talented celebrities who hone multiple crafts stand out for their hard work and dedication. Who are your favorite celebrities with multiple talents?
