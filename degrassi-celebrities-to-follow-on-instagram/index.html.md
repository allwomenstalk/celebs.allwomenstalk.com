# 7 "Degrassi: The Next Generation" Cast Members to Follow on Instagram

There are so many awesome 'Degrassi' celebrities that you should be following on Instagram! Of course, _Degrassi: The Next Generation_ has had many eras, and therefore many different actors and actresses have come out of that wildly successful franchise. On this list, we're keeping the spotlight on some of the most memorable **degrassi next generation cast** members. So no, you won't find Drake or Cassie Steele on this specific roundup! However, the **degrassi stars** listed here lead incredibly exciting lives and document their everyday adventures, creative projects, and nostalgic throwback memories online. Here is why following the **degrassi cast instagram** feeds is an absolute must for any fan of the show.

- Exclusive behind-the-scenes cast photos and throwback memories

- Personal style, fashion, and beauty inspiration

- Updates on new acting roles, music, and creative projects

## 1. Alexa Steele

![Alexa Steele](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/xq/rz/lzf5dea2_600x899.jpg)

Alexa is easily one of my favorite **degrassi cast members** to follow on Instagram because she is related to a former Degrassi legend and shares incredible content. Her feed features stunning photography, playful video clips, and sweet pictures alongside her sister, Cassie Steele. A fun fact that many fans surprisingly miss about **alexa steele degrassi** history: she originally played Angela Jeremiah in seasons 1 through 5 of _Degrassi: The Next Generation_ as Craig Manning's younger sister before returning later as Tori Santamaria! You can follow her adventures on Instagram at @alexasteele.

## 2. Christine Prosperi

![Christine Prosperi](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/f5/aq/tkjno3px_600x1143.jpg)

Christine portrayed one of my all-time favorite **degrassi the next generation characters**, the delightfully quirky Imogen Moreno. On social media, she frequently gives fans a peek behind the curtain with fun throwback set photos and candid moments. Just like her former castmate [Alexa Steele](#1), Christine keeps her feed vibrant and engaging. When she is not filming on set, she is consistently showing off effortlessly stylish outfits and sharing her thoughts on fashion, beauty, and daily life. You can keep up with her latest updates on Instagram at @crisprosperi.

## 3. Luke Bilyk

![Luke Bilyk](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/zq/jv/lfytou1e_411x594.jpg)

Let's be real, there were plenty of fan favorites on this series! While fans searching for **munro chambers instagram** updates might be disappointed to learn he keeps a low profile on social media, Luke Bilyk (who played Drew Torres) delivers everything you could want from **actors from degrassi**. You can follow him at @lukebilyk, where he regularly posts behind-the-scenes cast photos, travels around the globe, and fun reunions with fellow show alumni like [Christine Prosperi](#2).

## 4. Annie Clark

![Annie Clark](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/jh/u2/uy8l9ywh_600x872.jpg)

Even though Annie's time playing Fiona Coyne on the show has wrapped, she remains one of the top **degrassi actresses** to keep tabs on. Known as the iconic **blonde girl degrassi** fans adored for her dramatic plotlines and refined fashion sense, Annie made the move to California. Her Instagram profile (@annie__clark) is packed with gorgeous aesthetic photos, sun-drenched outdoor shots, and glimpses into her personal life after Degrassi.

## 5. Jessica Tyler

![Jessica Tyler](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/s4/br/c9fa52sp_554x728.jpg)

Jessica brought Jenna Middleton to life on Degrassi, and her character had a genuine charm that won over viewers throughout her arc. Beyond acting, Jessica is an immensely talented singer-songwriter with an amazing vocal range. Fans looking up **jessica tyler instagram** posts will enjoy her music clips, acoustic jam sessions, life updates, and uplifting motivational messages. She frequently stays connected with fellow alumni like [Annie Clark](#4) and [Luke Bilyk](#3). You can follow her official account at @jessicatylerofficial.

## 6. Melinda Shankar

![Melinda Shankar](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/kd/rp/my999tgx_500x749.jpg)

Mindy's feed is a treasure trove for anyone searching for **melinda shankar instagram** updates. She posts a dynamic mix of fitness motivation, outdoor workouts, behind-the-scenes memories from the Degrassi set, and warm selfies with her former co-stars. Following her account gives fans a genuine sense of connection to one of the show's most beloved stars. You can follow her at @mindyshankar.

## 7. Aislinn Paul

![Aislinn Paul](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/za/ss/blmwhd97_600x897.jpg)

Aislinn played Clare Edwards on Degrassi—the character so many fans rooted for through every emotional twist and turn (especially her legendary romance with Eli!). Her character development over the years was incredible to watch. If you are searching for **aislinn paul instagram** highlights, her profile (@aislinnclairee) is filled with artistic photography, cozy moments with friends, and reunions with co-stars like [Melinda Shankar](#6).
