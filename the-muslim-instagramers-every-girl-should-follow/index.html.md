# 7 Inspiring Muslim Instagrammers Every Woman Should Follow

Instagram has evolved into a premier platform for creative self-expression, featuring thousands of inspiring channels dedicated to makeup, fashion, fitness, and daily lifestyle. Among the standout creators on the platform are talented Muslim women who share incredible artistry, fashion-forward looks, and uplifting everyday content. These creators bring us expert makeup tutorials, high-fashion photography, versatile outfit ideas, and positive life encouragement. The best part is that anyone, regardless of background, can find value and inspiration in their work. Here is a curated list of seven standout Muslim Instagram creators every woman should follow to refresh her feed.

## 1. DRESSYOURFACE

![DRESSYOURFACE](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/o7/xg/id4p1xid_600x600.jpg)

Tamanna Roashan, known online as Dress Your Face, is a globally recognized beauty mogul and master makeup artist. Known for high-profile collaborations such as the popular Tamanna Palette with [Anastasia Beverly Hills](https://www.anastasiabeverlyhills.com), she consistently delivers detailed beauty transformation tutorials and skin care guidance on her page. Tamanna demonstrates how to elevate any look with precision, whether you are seeking full glam or a polished everyday appearance. Her impressive client list, featuring names like Lilly Ghalichi, Gretchen Rossi, and Leyla Milani, reflects her reputation in the beauty industry.

Following her account provides access to professional beauty masterclasses and technique breakdowns:

- Pro contouring and full-glam makeup tutorials
- Behind-the-scenes glimpses of celebrity beauty sessions
- Detailed product recommendations for all skill levels

If you appreciate expert technique and transformative beauty tips, adding Tamanna to your feed is an excellent step for your daily routine.

## 2. ROSEHESAN

![ROSEHESAN](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/g4/ot/hkw9g2dv_600x917.jpg)

Fashion is a powerful medium for personal expression, and Rose Hesan executes it with remarkable sophistication. As the creative force behind Warda Haute Couture, she designs elegant garments reminiscent of style icons across eras, from classic Hollywood glamour to modern runway aesthetic. Her profile offers an exclusive look into designer craftsmanship, featuring luxurious textiles, intricate detailing, and runway-ready silhouettes. For anyone looking for high-fashion inspiration and graceful tailoring, her page serves as a continuous source of creative ideas.

## 3. WITHLOVELEENA

![WITHLOVELEENA](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/th/bw/h3jvebrw_600x902.jpg)

Leena Snoubar offers a warm, personable approach to fashion and lifestyle blogging. Her content spans seasonal wardrobes, modest chic ensembles, home decor, and family life. Leena models versatile outfits that effortlessly bridge contemporary trends with modest elegance, making her feed accessible for diverse style preferences. Pairing her outfits with subtle [glamorous makeup technique](#1) ideas, she provides comprehensive inspiration for everyday dressing, accessories, and thoughtful living.

## 4. ARABBARBIE18

![ARABBARBIE18](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/r0/m0/imoi9ssx_422x700.jpg)

Eye makeup artistry takes center stage on Fatimah’s captivating profile. Through close-up photography and detailed aesthetic framing, she highlights complex shadow work, liner technique, and decorative accessories including elegant headpieces and statement jewelry pieces for women. Her creative blending of traditional cultural accents with modern cosmetics results in striking visual art. If you are passionate about detailed eye cosmetics, her feed is filled with inventive concepts to expand your skill set.

## 5. DEVOTEDLYYOURS

![DEVOTEDLYYOURS](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ml/lb/af0lxoyi_600x901.jpg)

Nuha brings a relatable, down-to-earth presence to modern style blogging. Balancing motherhood, family life, and fashion, she shares accessible outfit ideas that prioritize comfort without sacrificing style. Her recommendations focus on accessible high-street fashion, showing how to build a chic wardrobe on a budget while complementing your look with [effortless lifestyle aesthetics](#3).

| Creator Focus | Style Specialty | Target Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beauty & Glam | Pro cosmetics & masterclasses | Makeup enthusiasts |
| Modest Chic | High-street style & everyday wear | Fashion-conscious women |
| Wellness & Gym | Fitness routines & motivation | Active lifestyle seekers |

Her authentic communication style makes followers feel like they are getting advice from a close friend, making her account a delightful daily follow for budget-conscious fashion enthusiasts.

## 6. THATGIRLAMIRA

![THATGIRLAMIRA](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/ln/ka/rqan3u95_600x600.jpg)

Amira challenges conventional stereotypes in the wellness sphere by sharing her passion for strength training, fitness, and health. Her page features workout demonstrations, transformation milestones, and energetic fitness guidance tailored for women at all activity levels. She emphasizes consistency, proper lifting form, and balanced nutrition, making strength training approachable and empowering. If you want practical exercise routines and positive physical motivation on your timeline, Amira is a key creator to follow.

## 7. LIFELONGPERCUSSION

![LIFELONGPERCUSSION](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/jw/wg/yeotdd0d_600x631.jpg)

Rounding out the list is Lifelongpercussion, a creator celebrated for her vibrant personal aesthetic and flawless beauty styling. Known for precise eyebrow shaping, creative street style combinations, and expressive portraiture, her profile displays a distinctive sense of individuality. Her versatile fashion choices offer practical inspiration for women of any background, combining contemporary trends with personal flare. Much like [focused eye makeup artistry](#4), her attention to detail in every post makes her profile a standout destination.

These seven talented Muslim Instagram creators showcase the diversity of modern style, beauty artistry, and health. Following their journeys offers daily inspiration, practical fashion and beauty advice, and a fresh perspective for your social media feed. Be sure to check out their pages and add your favorites to your daily scroll.
