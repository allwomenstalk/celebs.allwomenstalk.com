# 7 Crush\-Worthy Celebrities Who Deserve Way More Attention

We tend to see the same Hollywood headliners over and over in magazines, but there are so many crush-worthy celebrities who deserve far more recognition. From hilarious comedy stars to multi-talented musicians and actors, these guys bring charm, immense talent, and undeniable looks to every role they take on. If you're looking for fresh stars to admire, look no further than this lineup. Before diving into our top picks, here is what makes these performers so captivating:

- Unmatched versatility across screen, stage, and music
- Genuine charisma and relatable, down-to-earth personalities
- Dedicated fanbases built on real talent rather than tabloid drama

## 1. Colton Haynes

![Colton Haynes](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/30203422_7090_500x688.jpg)

If you haven’t watched _Teen Wolf_ or _Arrow_, you’re seriously missing out. Colton Haynes is one of those crush-worthy celebrities who never appears less than picture-perfect. Having started his career as a professional model, he certainly knows how to command the camera in gorgeous photoshoots. Beyond his striking looks, his charm and openness about his life make him incredibly endearing to fans around the world.

## 2. Taran Killam

![Taran Killam](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31123724_3259_600x875.jpg)

Comedy fans searching for highlights from [Saturday Night Live](https://www.nbc.com/saturday-night-live) frequently look up Taran Killam (sometimes typed as taram killian by eager fans), and for good reason. As one of SNL’s most versatile cast members, he consistently delivered unforgettable laughs week after week. In his personal life, he is married to Cobie Smulders, known for her roles as Robin on _How I Met Your Mother_ and Maria Hill in _The Avengers_. While anyone looking up a cobie smulders boyfriend list will discover her deep relationship history, she and Taran remain one of the absolute cutest power couples in Hollywood, making them impossible not to love.

## 3. Reid Ewing

![Reid Ewing](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/30203421_3197_500x750.jpg)

If you watch [Modern Family](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1442437/), you immediately recognize Reid Ewing as Haley’s lovable, doofy boyfriend Dylan. Beyond his sharp comedic timing on television, Reid is also an accomplished singer-songwriter. Head over to YouTube to listen to his original comedic songs—if his witty lyrics don't bring a smile to your face, his classic handsome looks certainly will. For fans wondering is reid ewing married, he tends to keep his romantic life low-key, though admirers frequently notice reid ewing tattoo art and expressive style. How could anyone resist that face?

## 4. Zachary Levi

![Zachary Levi](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31130245_8956_600x831.jpg)

Anyone who watched the hit series _Chuck_ fell head over heels for Zachary Levi. He played the ultimate lovable nerd who won the heart of a super spy, proving his effortless charm. He also voiced the dashing Flynn Rider in Disney's _Tangled_ and stepped into the [Marvel Cinematic Universe](https://www.marvel.com) in _Thor: The Dark World_ (before starring in _Shazam!_). Watch any of his real-life interviews and you'll see he is just as warm, hilarious, and gentlemanly off camera as he is on screen.

## 5. Donald Glover

![Donald Glover](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31130307_8233_600x800.jpg)

Donald Glover captured hearts with his starring role as Troy on _Community_, but his brilliance extends far beyond acting. As a Golden Globe-winning writer, stand-up comedian, director, and Grammy-winning hip-hop artist (under the stage name Childish Gambino), he truly does it all. If you adore multi-talented creators—much like fellow actor-musician [Reid Ewing](#3)—Donald is the ultimate crush-worthy icon. Explore more in our [celebrity news and features](https://celebs.allwomenstalk.com/) for updates on multi-talented stars.

## 6. Chris Hardwick

![Chris Hardwick](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31130335_3188_600x817.jpg)

Stand-up comedian Chris Hardwick built a digital empire with Nerdist and won over television audiences hosting _The Talking Dead_. What makes him so adorable is his genuine enthusiasm for pop culture and fandoms. Much like fellow television personality [Taran Killam](#2), Chris embodies an infectious passion for entertainment. He feels like a fellow fan sharing in our favorite shows, paired with brilliant comic timing that makes him impossible not to root for.

## 7. Jake Johnson

![Jake Johnson](https://resize.allw.mn/1028x0/filters:format(webp)/filters:quality(70)/content/2013/12/31130437_7979_540x720.jpg)

Truthfully, every cast member of _New Girl_ deserves admiration, but Jake Johnson holds a special place in viewers' hearts as Nick Miller. With his charming smile, expressive eyes, and distinctive voice, he brings irresistible warmth to the screen. While photos showcase his handsome features, watching his dynamic comedic performances in _New Girl_ (and voice work in _Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse_) reveals the full extent of his magnetic charm.
