# What Nobody Tells You About Annoying Celebrities: 9 Habits We Hate

We love hearing about the latest celebrity hookups and breakups, getting some style inspiration, and trying out their latest workouts, but it is time to call attention to some of the most annoying things celebrities do. That’s right—irritating celebrities are guilty of doing some pretty frustrating things, and although I love pop culture as much as the next woman, I have to call them out on their annoying celebrity behavior!

## 1. Absolutely Fabulous

This has to be one of my top celebrity pet peeves: celebs swearing up and down that they are blessed with superior metabolisms that allow them to eat and drink pretty much everything under the sun without putting on a single pound! C’mon guys, there’s nothing wrong with admitting you work your butt off for that rock-hard bod or that you have to fight cravings every once in a while. In fact, I think everyone would respect you that much more and applaud your hard work and dedication!

## 2. Mom-agers

Mom-agers can be great—after all, who better to help manage your career than the person who knows you best? But some mom-agers take things a little too far, and that’s why it’s high on my list of annoying things celebrities do. From Dina Lohan to Kris Jenner, I get that they want to be in the limelight too, but let’s not get it twisted: don’t try to dress or act like your child/client, and keep it all business, not personal.

## 3. Dehydrated and Exhausted

Another one of the annoying celeb habits I have to bring up is when celebs claim they are constantly "dehydrated" or "exhausted" whenever they face scrutiny. Um, I get that you have a super busy schedule and you’re talented and all, but when is the last time you did half of the work of a working mother who can’t ever call in sick? Props to all the hard-working stars, but there are lots of regular folks in the exact same shoes doing it for much less!

Here are a few classic excuses we hear all too often:

- Attributing dramatic physical transformations solely to "drinking more water"

- Blaming extreme behavior on simple fatigue or dehydration

- Claiming hours of daily glam requires no effort at all

## 4. Perfect Pregnancy

There’s nothing like a slim and trim post-baby body to get us motivated, but another one of the annoying things celebrities do is claim that their entire pregnancy and delivery was a total breeze (looking at you, Gisele!) or that their bodies just magically bounced back after giving birth. Unlike those who talk about [superior metabolisms](#1), claiming a completely effortless transformation overlooks reality. I know some women have an easier time getting back to their pre-baby weight than others, but claiming a painless pregnancy and a six-pack just a few months after delivery feels a bit unrealistic!

## 5. Publicity Stunts

Ah, another entry on the list of top celebrity pet peeves is their shameless need for attention and over-the-top publicity stunts. Whether it’s a reality star getting caught up in a staged scandal or celebs insisting on being photographed in next to nothing, these tactics get old fast! Our everyday lives can get a little routine, but we’re not that desperate for staged entertainment.

| Celebrity Trend | Common Behavior | Why It Frustrates Fans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mom-agers | Over-managing careers | Stealing the spotlight from their kids |
| Publicity Stunts | Staged drama & scandals | Manufactured headlines for attention |
| Extravagant Spending | Multimillion-dollar splurges | Out-of-touch displays of wealth |

## 6. Reality Stars

So this isn’t exactly an annoying celebrity trait per se, but reality stars are a dime a dozen now, and for the most part, they’re famous for being drunk, acting crazy, or simply being good-looking—and that is not what makes a true star! The sudden influx of all these reality shows has gotten everyone confused as to what constitutes real talent versus what’s just temporary drama.

## 7. Pity Party

Some celebrities swear their bodies are perfect, while others are total negative Nancys, which is another totally annoying celeb habit. When Jessica Alba was expecting, she famously told [People magazine](https://people.com), “There’s no hiding the bump… they don’t make clothes for women with giant beer guts.” For the love of Pete, you’re carrying a child, not a beer gut! You can learn more about her wellness ventures at [The Honest Company](https://www.honest.com). Rather than staging [publicity stunts](#5) or feeling sorry for yourself, embrace the real changes that every expectant mother goes through!

## 8. Extravagant Spending

I get that celebs work really hard and deserve some retail therapy and then some, but some stars take their extravagant spending habits to another level, which is yet another one of the annoying things celebrities do. For example, Beyonce once rocked a pair of gold leggings from [Balenciaga](https://www.balenciaga.com) that cost her a cool $100k, and Victoria Beckham boasts a collection of luxury [Birkin bags](https://www.hermes.com) worth over $2 million! Call me crazy, but that’s a little much!

## 9. Stupid Sayings

This list of celebrity pet peeves wouldn’t be complete without mentioning their verbal diarrhea! We’re all guilty of uttering some embarrassing things once in a while, but celebrities should be used to people recording and hanging on their every word, so they should be a little more careful about what comes out of their mouths, right? Between outlandish [extravagant spending](#8) and tone-deaf remarks, celebs need to get it together!

There are a lot of annoying things celebrities do, but then again, they’re only human. There are many stars who do good, but their good deeds unfortunately get overshadowed by scandals and mishaps! What are your top celebrity pet peeves? Is there any celeb you love to hate?
