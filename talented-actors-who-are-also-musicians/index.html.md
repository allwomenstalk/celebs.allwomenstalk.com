# 9 Talented Actors Who Are Also Musicians \(And Surprisingly Good At It\)

I'm always surprised at how many actors who are also musicians walk among Hollywood's top talent. I'm not talking about just playing music in their garage with a few buddies on the weekend, either. Quite a few actors are gifted musicians, working as accomplished singers, guitarists, drummers, and songwriters with legitimate records and tours. Odds are, you've probably listened to songs from some of these actors in bands without even realizing who was behind the mic or instrument.

## 1. Keanu Reeves

When I think of Keanu Reeves, I instantly want to watch "The Matrix" or "John Wick." As it turns out, he was also the dedicated bassist for the alternative rock and grunge band Dogstar for over a decade. Somehow it's not all that surprising he's among the slew of actors who make music with genuine passion. You can learn more about his screen career on his [official IMDb page](https://www.imdb.com/name/nm0000206/). If you're into 90s grunge and melodic rock, you should definitely check out Dogstar's discography, which showcases his impressive rhythm section skills alongside other [actors in bands](#5) who take their craft seriously.

## 2. Ryan Gosling

Is it really a shock to find out that Hollywood star Ryan Gosling is also a talented singer-songwriter? Believe it or not, "The Notebook" and "Barbie" actor is the frontman and co-founder of Dead Man's Bones. Categorized under dark indie rock, their music features a hauntingly beautiful folk sound paired with a youth choir. Discovering his rich vocal ability proves that he is truly one of the most versatile actors who are gifted musicians, much like his fellow performer [Zooey Deschanel](#3) who seamlessly bridges cinema and melody.

## 3. Zooey Deschanel

Zooey Deschanel is far more than the hilarious Jess from "New Girl." She forms half of the critically acclaimed indie folk-pop duo She & Him alongside M. Ward. Beyond her lead vocals, she is a multi-instrumentalist who plays piano, guitar, ukulele, and pedal steel. Visit the [official She & Him website](https://www.sheandhim.com/) to hear her soothing retro arrangements. She remains a standout example of actors who are good musicians with true artistic depth.

## 4. Jason Schwartzman

Even though he eventually stepped back to focus on acting, Jason Schwartzman originally served as the drummer for the indie rock band Phantom Planet. Their iconic track "California" became famous around the world as the theme song for "The O.C." He hasn't left his musical passion behind, continuing as a solo artist under the moniker Coconut Records. His background confirms that many musician actors possess deep roots in the music industry long before their biggest film roles take off.

## 5. Kevin Bacon

It's a family affair when it comes to Kevin Bacon's musical output. He teamed up with his brother Michael to form The Bacon Brothers, blending folk, rock, soul, and country music into a distinct americana sound. The "Footloose" and "City on a Hill" star has recorded multiple albums and toured extensively over several decades. Check out their releases on [The Bacon Brothers official site](https://baconbrothers.com/) to hear why he ranks among the most dedicated singers who are also actors working today.

## 6. Hugh Laurie

If you've ever watched an episode of "House," you probably noticed Hugh Laurie displaying remarkable skill on the piano and guitar. Those musical moments weren't dubbed—they were entirely genuine. An accomplished pianist, vocalist, and blues guitarist, Laurie has released celebrated blues albums such as "Let Them Talk" and "Didn't It Rain." Blues fans should definitely sample his catalog, which highlights a soulful contrast to his famous TV persona and proves he is a stellar example of actors that make music with genuine artistry.

## 7. Clint Eastwood

Many fans are surprised to discover Clint Eastwood's deep musical credentials. While best known as a legendary cinema icon, he is also a passionate jazz pianist and film composer. Eastwood has composed scores and themes for several of his acclaimed films, including "Mystic River," "Million Dollar Baby," and "Grace Is Gone." Beyond directing and acting, his background as a composer shows that talented actors often channel their creativity into classical and jazz composition.

## 8. Michael Pitt

Some actors immerse themselves so thoroughly in a role that it transforms their real life. Michael Pitt, known for "Boardwalk Empire" and "Last Days," served as the lead vocalist and guitarist for the grunge band Pagoda. The group released raw, high-energy albums that appealed to fans of 90s alternative rock. His work with Pagoda demonstrates how actors who also make music often bring raw passion and authentic stage presence to their live performances.

Here are key skills that bridge acting and musical performances for top stars:

- Vocal expression and pitch control
- Stage presence and audience connection
- Rhythmic timing and improvisation
- Emotional storytelling through lyrics

## 9. Donnie Wahlberg

Long before starring in "Blue Bloods," Donnie Wahlberg skyrocketed to fame as a founding member of New Kids On The Block. The pop group sold tens of millions of records worldwide and defined an era of pop culture. After stepping into acclaimed acting roles in film and television, Wahlberg continues to tour and record with NKOTB. He stands out as one of the most successful musicians who are actors, balancing arena tours with a full-time television series.
